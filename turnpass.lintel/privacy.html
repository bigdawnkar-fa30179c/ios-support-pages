Privacy Policy

Turn Pass keeps the wall, the chuck shoulder, and the spindle speed for a blank you are turning. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The blank, its length and radius, and each pass you drag along it are stored on this iPhone. Turn Pass does not ask you to create an account.

What Turn Pass does not collect

Turn Pass does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
