Turn Pass

Turn Pass follows one blank between centers. Drag a pass, then read the wall, the chuck shoulder, and the speed.

A blank

Mount the length and the radius. The bore is already in the wood. The first inch has to stay thick enough for the chuck.

A pass

Drag along the wood to take a pass. A wall under 0.12 in is about to show the bore. The speed line shows the rpm that holds about 1000 surface feet at the current diameter.

On this phone

The blank and its passes stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
