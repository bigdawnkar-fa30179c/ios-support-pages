Skewback

Skewback lays out one masonry opening. Drag the crown, then read the skewback.

The opening

The span, the rise, and the stone depth set the ring. The middle stone is the keystone. The two stones on the piers are the skewbacks.

The cut

Drag the crown up or down. Under 28 degrees the springing stones turn red: they are too flat to cut. A soffit under 1.5 inches is too narrow.

On this phone

The openings stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
