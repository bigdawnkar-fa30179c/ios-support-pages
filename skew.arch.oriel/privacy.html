Privacy Policy

Skewback lays out one masonry opening. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The opening, its span and rise, the stone depth, and the number of stones are stored on this iPhone. Skewback does not ask you to create an account.

What Skewback does not collect

Skewback does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
