Cycleglass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Cycleglass does not collect personal information.

Tanks, water tests, photos, and water changes stay on your iPhone. There is no account or sign-in. The app does not include analytics, ads, or third-party account services.

The camera and photo library are used only if you add a photo of the test vials. That photo stays on this iPhone.

If you export a CSV and tap Share, that file is handed to the app you choose.

Notifications, if you turn them on, are scheduled on this iPhone for a water-change reminder and for test-tube timers.

Deleting the app deletes what you saved.
