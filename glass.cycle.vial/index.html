Cycleglass

Cycleglass keeps a water log for a freshwater tank.

Time the test tubes, match the color, and read Safe, Watch, or Act against the limits you set. Nitrate tests estimate the next water change. A new tank shows which stage of the cycle it is in.

On this phone

Tanks, readings, photos, and water changes stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. A CSV leaves the app only when you share it.
