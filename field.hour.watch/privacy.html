Privacy Policy

Fieldhour Privacy

Fieldhour stores your watches, sites, and taps on this iPhone. There is no account.

If you allow location, Fieldhour reads it once to place a meteor radiant in your sky and saves that position with the site. You can type the latitude and longitude instead.

Sharing a report sends only the text you choose to share, through the system share sheet.

Deleting the app deletes the watches stored by Fieldhour.
