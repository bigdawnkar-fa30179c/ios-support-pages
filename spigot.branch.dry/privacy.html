Privacy Policy

Goes dry shows which taps lose water when a valve is closed. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The building, its valves, its taps, and how they connect are stored on this iPhone. Goes dry does not ask you to create an account.

What Goes dry does not collect

Goes dry does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
