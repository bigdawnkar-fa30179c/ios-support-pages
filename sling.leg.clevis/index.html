Bridle

Bridle hangs one load on two equal sling legs. Drag the lugs and the center, then read each leg.

The legs

The hook sits between the two lugs. Spreading the lugs lowers the hook and raises the tension. An angle under 30 degrees from horizontal is too flat.

The tag

The tag is the rated load for one leg. A bridle uses that rating. A choker uses three quarters of it. A basket uses twice that rating.

On this phone

Picks stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. This picture is not an engineering stamp. Read the sling tag and the crane chart before a pick.
