Privacy Policy

Bridle shows the tension in each leg of a two-leg sling. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The pick name, the load weight, the sling tag, the leg length, the lug positions, and the center of the load are stored on this iPhone. Bridle does not ask you to create an account.

What Bridle does not collect

Bridle does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
