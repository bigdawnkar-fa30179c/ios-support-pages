Privacy Policy

Crack Gauge keeps crack-gauge readings for one wall. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The wall, its photo, the gauges, and each reading are stored on this iPhone. Crack Gauge does not ask you to create an account.

What Crack Gauge does not collect

Crack Gauge does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy. The camera and photo library are used only when you add a photo of the wall. That photo stays on this iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
