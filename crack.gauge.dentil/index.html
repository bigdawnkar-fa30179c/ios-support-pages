Crack Gauge

Crack Gauge keeps monthly readings from a tell-tale on a wall.

A reading

Place a gauge on the wall photo. On each visit, drag the crosshair to the mark you see, or type the numbers. Width points use the width card.

The trend

After enough visits, Crack Gauge separates a seasonal open-and-close from a crack that keeps widening, and places the widest point on the BRE 251 scale.

On this phone

Walls, gauges, readings, and photos stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. A report leaves the app only when you share it.
