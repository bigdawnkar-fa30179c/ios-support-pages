Broodline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Broodline does not collect personal information.

Yards, hives, inspection rounds, and photos stay on your iPhone. There is no account or sign-in. The app does not include analytics, ads, or third-party account services.

The camera and photo library are used only if you add a photo of a frame to a round. That photo stays on this iPhone.

If you tap Share a note, the text of that round is handed to the app you choose.

Notifications, if you allow them, are scheduled on this iPhone to remind you when a hive is due to be opened.

Deleting the app deletes what you saved.
