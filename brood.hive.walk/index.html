Broodline

Broodline keeps hive inspection notes for a beekeeper walking a yard.

Hives stand in the order you walk them. Mark each frame, note the queen and eggs, and enter the mite wash. Broodline turns the wash into mites per 100 bees and marks a hive when it is time to control mites. It does not pick a treatment or give doses.

On this phone

Yards, hives, rounds, and photos stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. A note leaves the app only when you share it.
