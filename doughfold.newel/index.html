Proof Fold

Proof Fold sets the gap between folds from the dough temperature, then keeps each stamp you actually make.

A dough

Start a dough with its temperature and the flour weight. At 22°C the gap is 50 minutes. At 26°C it is 30. Between those, the gap moves with the temperature.

A fold

Stamp a fold when you fold. A stamp before 70% of the gap is early. A stamp after 140% is late. The next window shows when the following fold belongs.

On this phone

The loaf and its stamps stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.
