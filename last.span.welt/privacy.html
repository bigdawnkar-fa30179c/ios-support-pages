Privacy Policy

Last Fit compares a traced foot with a shoe last. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The traced outline, the foot length, and the last you pick are stored on this iPhone. Last Fit does not ask you to create an account.

What Last Fit does not collect

Last Fit does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
