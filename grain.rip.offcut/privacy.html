Ripstock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Ripstock does not collect personal information.

Jobs, parts, and offcuts stay on your iPhone. There is no account or sign-in. The app does not include analytics, ads, or third-party account services.

If you export a PDF and tap Share, that file is handed to the app you choose.

Deleting the app deletes what you saved.
