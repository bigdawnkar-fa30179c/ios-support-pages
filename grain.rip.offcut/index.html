Ripstock

Ripstock is a sheet cut planner for the shop.

List the parts you need and the sheets you have. Ripstock packs them with straight through-cuts, allows for the blade kerf and any edge trim, and will not turn a part when you lock the grain.

Saw mode walks the job one cut at a time. Finished offcuts stay on a rack for the next job in the same material and thickness.

On this phone

Jobs, parts, and offcuts stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. A PDF leaves the app only when you share it.
